Miso Lee

I am a Ph.D. student in Visual Computing Lab at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), advised by Prof. Jae-Pil Heo. I work on 3D vision, especially visual localization and 3D scene representations. More broadly, I am interested in representation learning for geometric reasoning. I was a research intern at NAVER Cloud in the summer of 2026, working on 4D human-scene reconstruction.
